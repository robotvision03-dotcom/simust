Privacy Policy — My SIMUST

Last updated: 11 September 2026 · App package com.simust.mysimust

My SIMUST (“the App”) is published by SIMUST / robotvision03 for football training players. The App is a thin client that opens the My SIMUST web portal in a secure WebView.

1. Data we process

Account data you enter on the portal (for example username, password, name, club, bookings, and training results) is processed on SIMUST servers to provide login, reservations, progress, and results. The App itself stores only local preferences on your device (host URL, text size, screen orientation, keep-screen-on).

2. How data is used

Data is used solely to operate the SIMUST training service: authentication, session unlocks, calendar reservations, and displaying your results. We do not sell personal data to third parties for advertising.

3. Network access

The App requires internet access to reach the My SIMUST host (by default https://my.simust.com). Traffic goes to SIMUST servers. Payment for reservations (when enabled) is handled by Stripe through the portal, not by a separate in-app billing SDK.

4. Permissions

  • Internet / network state — load the portal and show connection status.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location permissions.

5. Retention

Account and training records are kept on SIMUST servers for as long as your academy/operator maintains the service. Local preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall.

6. Children

My SIMUST is intended for academy players under coach/operator supervision. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor’s account was created inappropriately, contact us using the address below.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@simust.com · Product site: https://simust.com · Portal: https://my.simust.com/login

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the App or portal changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change; continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
